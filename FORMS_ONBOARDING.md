**FORMS (ONBOARDING)**

**Property Information Form** -- Developer enters project details
(location, pricing, amenities, ROI, possession, etc.).

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Form** -- Define the target buyer so the
AI can create a digital twin.

**Lead Qualification Form** -- Used by sales teams to qualify incoming
leads.

**Developer Onboarding Form** -- For builders onboarding their projects.

**AI Lead Generation Configuration Form** -- Configure how the AI
searches, scores, and nurtures leads.

**Buyer Inquiry Form** -- Website form for potential buyers.

**CRM Lead Intake Form** -- Internal form for adding and managing leads.
