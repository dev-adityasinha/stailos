**AI Outputs Generated from This Form**

Once submitted, the AI can automatically generate:

1.  **Ideal Customer Digital Twin (ICP)** with demographics, income
    bands, occupations, locations, interests, and buying behavior.

2.  **Buyer Personas** for segments such as HNIs, NRIs, CXOs, investors,
    and first-time buyers.

3.  **Lead Scoring Rules** to prioritize prospects based on
    affordability and purchase likelihood.

4.  **Marketing Campaigns** including Meta, Google, LinkedIn, email,
    WhatsApp, and SMS creatives.

5.  **Landing Pages** tailored to different buyer personas.

6.  **Sales Playbooks** with objection handling, follow-up sequences,
    and site visit strategies.

7.  **Competitor Intelligence Reports** comparing pricing, positioning,
    and USPs.

8.  **Predictive Analytics** for expected lead quality, booking
    probability, campaign performance, and projected ROI.

9.  **CRM Configuration** with custom fields, automations, pipelines,
    and AI-powered lead routing.

10. **Executive Dashboard** showing project health, marketing KPIs, lead
    funnel metrics, and revenue forecasts.
