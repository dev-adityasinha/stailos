**🤖 What Happens After Submission?**

Once the form is submitted, the AI platform can automatically:

**1. Create a Buyer Profile**

Build a comprehensive customer profile based on the buyer\'s budget,
preferences, location, and objectives.

**2. Generate a Personalized Property Match**

Recommend the most suitable projects from the developer\'s inventory
based on the buyer\'s criteria.

**3. Calculate Affordability**

Estimate financing options, EMI ranges, and affordability based on the
buyer\'s budget and loan preferences.

**4. Assign a Lead Score**

Evaluate purchase readiness using declared preferences, budget
alignment, and inquiry details.

**5. Personalize Communication**

Generate customized WhatsApp messages, emails, brochures, and investment
summaries relevant to the buyer.

**6. Schedule Follow-ups**

Create reminders for the sales team and automate follow-up sequences
according to the buyer\'s preferred contact method and timeline.

**7. Book Site Visits**

Coordinate site visit requests, notify sales representatives, and
confirm appointments with the buyer.

**8. Update the CRM**

Create or update the lead record, assign it to the appropriate sales
pipeline, and maintain a complete interaction history.

**9. Deliver Investment Insights**

Provide ROI estimates, payment plan options, location advantages, and
relevant project highlights tailored to the buyer\'s goals.

**10. Continuously Improve Recommendations**

As the buyer interacts with the website, opens emails, responds on
WhatsApp, or revisits projects, the AI refines recommendations and
updates the buyer\'s profile to improve future interactions.
