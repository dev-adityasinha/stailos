**🤖 AI Agents Configured by This Form**

After configuration, the platform can automatically deploy:

**1. Customer Discovery Agent**

Builds Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) and identifies high-potential
audience segments using approved data sources and first-party signals.

**2. Lead Qualification Agent**

Scores every lead based on demographics, affordability, intent,
engagement, and fit.

**3. Lead Routing Agent**

Assigns leads to the appropriate salesperson or workflow according to
configurable rules.

**4. Personalization Agent**

Generates tailored emails, WhatsApp messages, landing pages, and
marketing creatives for each buyer persona.

**5. Sales Assistant Agent**

Schedules follow-ups, handles common objections, proposes next-best
actions, and keeps the CRM updated.

**6. Marketing Optimization Agent**

Monitors campaign performance, recommends improvements, and automates
optimizations where enabled.

**7. Analytics & Forecasting Agent**

Provides dashboards for lead quality, conversion rates, revenue
forecasts, campaign ROI, and sales pipeline health.

**8. Learning Agent**

Continuously improves lead scoring and personalization by learning from
closed deals, lost opportunities, and customer engagement---using your
organization\'s own historical data and consented interactions.
