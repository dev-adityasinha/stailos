**AI Outputs Generated from This Form**

After submission, the AI can automatically produce:

-   **Ideal Customer Digital Twin** with demographic, behavioral,
    financial, and psychographic attributes.

-   **Lookalike Audience Models** using first-party CRM data and
    advertising platform capabilities (rather than scraping restricted
    platforms).

-   **Lead Scoring Model** that ranks prospects by affordability,
    intent, and likelihood to convert.

-   **Personalized Messaging Frameworks** tailored by persona,
    occupation, and motivation.

-   **Audience Segments** (e.g., CXOs, doctors, founders, NRIs, HNIs,
    investors).

-   **Channel Recommendations** identifying the most effective marketing
    channels for each segment.

-   **Campaign Strategy** with audience definitions, creative angles,
    offers, and budget allocation.

-   **Sales Playbooks** with likely objections, buying triggers, and
    recommended follow-up sequences.

-   **Predictive Insights** on conversion probability, expected CPL,
    booking likelihood, and estimated customer lifetime value.

-   **CRM Automation Rules** for routing, prioritization, nurturing, and
